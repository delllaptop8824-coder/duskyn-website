Who We Are

About DUSKYN

DUSKYN, operated by DUSKYN APPARELS LLP, provides online sourcing assistance for businesses and individuals seeking products and manufacturing opportunities in India and China.

Whether you need help finding a suitable manufacturer in India, sourcing products from China, or identifying suppliers for your business requirements, DUSKYN aims to make the sourcing process more accessible and convenient.

Our focus is on understanding your requirements and helping you explore suitable sourcing solutions.

Online sourcing services since 2017 DUSKYN has been providing online sourcing services since 2017.

DUSKYN is operated by DUSKYN APPARELS LLP, based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India.
